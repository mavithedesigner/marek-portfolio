Artistic Area Club started because music, design and art around here mostly ran
in separate lanes. The brand had to say that out loud without turning into a
members-only badge.

## One idea, held to

The mark came from watching separate liquids poured together until they set as
one shape. The letters run into each other instead of lining up.

That sounds poetic until you have to print it on a 9 mm keyring. Most of the
work was not the idea, it was keeping the idea legible at every size it had to
survive.

## What it had to pass

- **A phone screen at arm's length.** If the letters merge into a blob, it fails.
- **A black t-shirt, one colour.** No gradients to hide behind.
- **A poster seen from across a room**, next to five other logos.

## What I would change

The three-dimensional version came later and it does a lot of work on social
media. If I started again I would design the flat one and the dimensional one at
the same time, not one after the other.

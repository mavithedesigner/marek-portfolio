eDesk is where Slovak citizens and companies receive official documents from the
state. When the platform became mandatory, every rough edge in it stopped being
an inconvenience and turned into a problem for millions of people at once.

## The thing nobody tells you about scale

On a small project, a confusing label is a small problem. One person gets stuck,
writes to support, moves on. On a service used by millions, the same label is a
different animal entirely.

> A sentence that reads as slightly unclear to you will be read as completely
> unclear by thousands of people on the same morning.

That reframing changed how I argue for decisions. Not "I think this is nicer",
but "this wording sends about four hundred people to a phone line".

### Three habits I picked up

- **Research before opinion.** On a project this size, taste is the cheapest and
  least useful thing in the room.
- **Write the reason down.** If a decision cannot survive one sentence of
  explanation, it will not survive a stakeholder meeting either.
- **Design for the worst case.** The person opening a legal notice on a phone in
  a queue, not the person at a 27-inch monitor with a coffee.

I was the junior in the room. I would recommend it.

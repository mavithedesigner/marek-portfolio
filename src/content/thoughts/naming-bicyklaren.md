Most briefs arrive with the name already decided and the designer is asked to
make it look good. Bicykľáreň went the other way round, and that changed
everything that came after.

## The name did the hard part

It is a bike rental inside a resort. The name had to sound local, be easy to say
out loud on the phone, and not fight the parent brand it belongs to.

Once the name was right, the mark almost designed itself: **one continuous
stroke**, the way a bike rolls, with no bicycle icon anywhere near it.

> If you need an icon of the thing to explain the thing, the name is not pulling
> its weight.

## Where the time actually went

- Three days on **names**
- An afternoon on the **wordmark**
- Two weeks on the **boring parts**: the secondary lockup, the sign above the
  door, the booking flow

That ratio surprises clients every time, and it is the honest one.

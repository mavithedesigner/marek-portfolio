The question I get is always some version of "does it design for you". No. It
does the work around the design, which is most of the work.

## Where it earns its place

- **Second opinions at midnight.** Explaining a decision out loud is how you find
  out it is wrong, and it does not need to be a human listening.
- **The boring text.** Alt text, meta descriptions, the forty microcopy strings
  nobody budgets for.
- **Research you would otherwise skip.** Fifteen competitor sites summarised in
  the time it takes to open five of them.

## Where it does not

- Anything that needs **taste**. It will happily give you the average answer, and
  the average answer is the one nobody remembers.
- Anything that needs **a client's trust**. That is built in meetings, not in
  prompts.

## The honest version

It made me **faster at the parts I like least**, which means more hours on the
parts clients actually pay for. That is the whole story. Anyone selling you more
than that is selling something.

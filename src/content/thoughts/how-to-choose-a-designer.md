Most people choose a designer by looking at pictures. It is the obvious thing to
do and it is also the reason so many projects go sideways.

## Ask what the work was for

A logo on a dark background with a nice shadow tells you nothing. Ask what the
business needed, what changed afterwards, and what the designer would do
differently now. Anyone who cannot answer the third question has not thought
about their own work very hard.

## Ask who else was involved

Design is rarely a solo act. Knowing whether someone led a project or helped on
it is not a trap, it is context. A person who is honest about this is a person
who will be honest about a deadline.

## Ask about the parts that went badly

Every real project has a week where it is not working. The answer tells you how
someone behaves when a client is nervous, which is the part you are actually
hiring.

## Ask what they need from you

Good work needs decisions, access and time. A designer who asks for nothing will
deliver something that looks fine and fits nothing.
